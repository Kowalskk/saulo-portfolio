                          How it is measured

                          • Assistance Fund and bot: Hyperliquid ledger (USD value when sent) and the bot's fills on the KNTQ/USDC pair (@334). The fund's balance equals the sum of sends.
                          • Ascend: KNTQ transfers into and out of its burn treasury on Elysium, with what each buy paid. Matches stateofblocks.com to the token.
                          • Montra: converter sales, treasury buys (swaps inside the pool, which emit no KNTQ transfer) and MONTRA burned. stateofblocks only counts the automatic buyback.
                          • Sequencer: daily rise of its fee account balance, which has never sent anything.
                          • Burns on Elysium: KNTQ sent to 0x0 or 0x…dEaD, without bridge withdrawals. Explorer verifications are KNTQ transferAndCall calls to 0x…dEaD.
                          • Staking: withdrawal events of the sKNTQ contract 0x696238e0ca31c94e24ca4cbe7921754e172e4d0f on HyperEVM; the sKNTQ value is KNTQ paid per sKNTQ on each withdrawal.
                          • New supply: KNTQ campaigns from Merkl's public API, claims from its distributor on Elysium, and HyperEVM transfers of the genesis wallets.
                          • Pledges: Kinetiq's table, the official Elysium directory (115 projects) and the projects' own posts; new ones are tracked automatically.
                          • Supply, staking and price: Kinetiq's and Hyperliquid's public APIs.
                          • Everything is regenerated daily at 06:10 UTC on our server and uploaded afterwards.
